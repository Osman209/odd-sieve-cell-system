The Cell System

A coordinate system for the odd sieve — what it proves, what it measures, and where it stops

Mohamed Osman · ORCID 0009-0004-5912-999X · independent researcher

Papers I–IV build a construction and prove what it decides. Paper V derives the point at which it stops from inside the construction rather than quoting it, and measures how much of the difficulty the framework can localise before failing to cross it. Every headline result reached here already exists in the literature, and each paper names the source. No progress toward the twin-prime conjecture is claimed, and no new bound. Priority is not claimed for any result.

What the collection offers instead: a construction that produces Buchstab's identity, the Hardy–Littlewood singular series and the sifting limit without importing them and then stops exactly where the field stops; negative results and withdrawn claims kept rather than deleted; and a script in code/ that regenerates every number printed.
