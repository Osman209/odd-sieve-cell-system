Paper 6. The Twin Criterion: Six Exception Positions

A single open cell in a sector is a twin pair unless it sits at one of six named places

Mohamed Osman · ORCID 0009-0004-5912-999X · independent researcher · 2026-09-07

Download the PDF · source on GitHub · concept DOI

Abstract. After switching on every line up to M in the sector between M^2 and (M+6)^2, at most six cells can be open without being a twin, and their positions are explicit quadratics in M.

No progress toward the twin-prime conjecture is claimed, and no new bound. Priority is not claimed for any result. Every number printed is regenerated by a script in code/ of the repository. Licence: CC BY 4.0 (text), MIT (code).
